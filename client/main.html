What is warframe?

Warframe is a free-to-play science fantasy hack-and-slash game featuring space ninjas.

Players can fund the game by purchasing in-game currency called Platinum (or Plat), but the in-game economy allows for trade, enabling free accounts to farm items and trade them for Platinum. Plat is used to accelerate timers, buy in-game cosmetics, and trade for most things. Free players will need to dip their toes in the market if they progress long enough.

The deep lore involves children possessing robots called warframes (space ninjas) made from an infested (enemy faction) strain.

The core gameplay loop is go to a mission, clear the objective(s), gather materials, and craft better gear.

Clan?

A clan is like a guild: a collection of players gathered around a shared purpose. Some clans will only let you join if you are very hardcore about farming / grinding or event focused. Some are big into collecting taxes. Here's us in a nutshell:

  • 0% tax rate: the clan will never force members to give up credits or materials.
  • Casual: the clan does not force members to log in frequently. Real life comes first. Some members play a lot some do not.
  • Mentorship on request: if you request advice it is available. We have veterans who have been playing the game for years and we have people who are brand new.
  • Discord: We use Discord to form squads and coordinate.

My oldest started playing on the XBox so don't assume Wurmboy1 is me.

We are a family friendly clan for casual gamers that aren't focused on efficency or the hardcore grind. We have a few players who do play like that but the clan wants to stay relaxed.

Advice

  • Excalibur Umbra is very good for free players. There are several quests to clear to get it but it is worth it.
  • It's good to know the current Market Values of items if you're looking to trade.
  • We throw around a lot of slang. For example Orokin Catalysts we refer to as potatoes.

Stay Free-to-Play

Get Prime Blue Prints

Relics are awarded on several missions.

Initiate a relic mission using the relic alerts.

Relics have a random chance of containing one of a variety of components.

You can choose from the relics opened by all squad members; if someone draws a rare item, everyone can claim it.

Sell Primes for platinum

Sell items to other players through the trade chat.

Use market sites to know the going rate of things and not be scammed.

Lean more about trading via the wiki

Buy What You Want

Utilize the trade chat again to purchase the items you want or just spend the platinum on things in the market or cosmetics.

Gear Suggestions

Gear suggestions are not authoratative, just tips if you're lost in a sea of choices. There are lots of good alternatives, too.

Frames

Free accounts start off with 3 frame slots. Don't feel like you need to collect them all. Every frame can do everything, but some frames are better at some things.

Excalibur Umbra

Excalibur Umbra is given to you after you complete The Sacrifice quest and is verry good at crowd control.

Rhino

Rhino is a good jack of all trades.

Frost

Frost is good for defense missions.

Nekros

Nekros is good for survival missions.

Loki

Loki is good for spy missions.

Zephyr

Zephyr is good for farming open worlds like Plains of Eidolon, Orb Vallis, and Cambion Drift.

Primary

Soma

Free players should work on getting the Soma if they like rifles. Large magazine, fast fire rate, good crit chance.

Boltor

If you like rifles but dont have the Mastery Rank (MR) for the soma, I recommend the Boltor.

Phantasma

The Phantasma is a laser shotgun with high status chance.

Fulmin

The Fulmin has two fire modes, a rifle and a shotgun.

Secondary

Atomos

The Atomos is a good weapon for status effects.

Melee

Broken War

The Broken War is given to you after you complete The Second Dream quest, it is one of the best melee weapons you can get.

Skiajati

The Skiajati is given to you after you complete The Sacrifice quest and it is a very good Nikana.
